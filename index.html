La Nostra Storia

From a small Florence trattoria to the heart of Mayfair

In 1987, Nonna Serafina De Luca opened a twelve-table trattoria in Florence with a single belief: that the finest cooking is simply great ingredients, treated with love. Three generations later, her grandson — Head Chef Marco De Luca — carries that same philosophy to London.

Every pasta is made by hand, every sauce built slowly, every wine chosen from the region it belongs to. It is cooking without shortcuts — and hospitality without pretense.
