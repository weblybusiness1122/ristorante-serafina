La Nostra Storia

Our Story

Three generations, one belief: that the finest cooking is simply great ingredients, treated with love — and shared like family.

Since 1987

Born in Florence, at home in Mayfair

It began with twelve tables and a hand-painted sign. In the spring of 1987, Nonna Serafina De Luca opened a small trattoria on a cobbled Florentine street, cooking the recipes her own grandmother had taught her — ragù that simmered from dawn, pasta rolled by hand each morning, and a welcome so warm that guests became regulars, and regulars became family.

Nearly four decades later, her grandson Marco carries that same spirit to London. Our Mayfair dining room is a tribute to those origins: a room of white Carrara marble, soft candlelight and quiet gold, where the pace is unhurried and every plate still begins with the question Nonna always asked — "Is it good enough for someone you love?"

L'Atmosfera

An evening that feels like Italy

Step inside and the city falls away. Marble underfoot, the low glow of candles, the hush of linen and crystal, and the gentle clatter of an open kitchen. Barolo is decanted at the table, focaccia arrives warm from the oven, and the espresso machine hums late into the night. It is grand without being formal — the kind of room where a first date, a fortieth birthday and a quiet Tuesday dinner all feel equally at home.

Whether you are gathered around our marble bar for an Aperol Spritz before dinner, or lingering over limoncello long after the plates are cleared, time at Serafina is meant to be savoured — con calma, as we say.

What We Stand For

Four things we never compromise

Ingredients

San Marzano tomatoes, Parmigiano aged 24 months, '00' flour and oil pressed in Puglia — imported direct from the source.

Craft

Pasta rolled by hand each morning, sauces built slowly, pizza fired in a wood oven on 48-hour dough. No shortcuts, ever.

Hospitality

Every guest is welcomed like family. Attentive, never intrusive — the quiet art of making you feel completely at home.

Heritage

Recipes passed down three generations, cooked today exactly as Nonna Serafina intended — honest, regional, timeless.

The Kitchen

Led by Chef Marco De Luca

Trained in the kitchens of Bologna and Modena before returning to the family table, Marco cooks with a reverence for tradition and a restless eye for detail. His philosophy is simple: respect the ingredient, honour the region, and let the produce speak.

You will often find him at the pass late in the evening, finishing a risotto with a final knob of butter, or carrying a plate to the table himself — because, as he says, "a dish is a letter you write to the person eating it."

From Our Table

Dishes worth travelling for

A handful of the plates and pours that have become quietly famous. Discover the full list — with every regional detail — on our menu.

Tagliatelle al Ragù · Bologna

Our most-ordered plate. Hand-rolled egg ribbons folded through a ragù of beef and pork that simmers for six hours, finished with aged Parmigiano.

Risotto ai Funghi e Tartufo · Piemonte

Carnaroli rice stirred to a slow, creamy perfection with wild mushrooms and shavings of black truffle — the dish guests whisper about.

Linguine allo Scoglio · Amalfi

Clams, mussels and king prawns with cherry tomato and a splash of white wine — a taste of the southern coast on a London evening.

Ossobuco alla Milanese · Milano

Veal shank braised until it falls from the bone, brightened with gremolata and set beside a golden saffron risotto.

Bistecca alla Fiorentina · Toscana

A dry-aged T-bone grilled over fire with nothing more than rosemary and sea salt — Tuscany, uncomplicated and generous, to share.

Tiramisù della Casa · Veneto

Mascarpone and espresso-soaked savoiardi layered and dusted with bitter cocoa — made to Nonna's original recipe, every day.

Margherita DOP · Napoli

San Marzano, fior di latte and basil on 48-hour dough, blistered in our wood oven. Proof that simplicity, done perfectly, is luxury.

Aperitivo & the Cellar · tutta Italia

From a bittersweet Aperol Spritz to a contemplative glass of Barolo — a list that travels every region of Italy, poured with care.
